YOUR PART: the near term (the first months). Another writer covers the multi-year phases.

- headline: one sentence describing the overall plan.
- current_job_heading: "In your current job as <title>" if they have a current job (from the profile or resume); otherwise "Where you are now".
- current_job_moves: 3 to 5 specific things they can do INSIDE their current job (or current situation, e.g. school or volunteering, if they have no job) to move toward the first role on the path that they do not already hold. Examples: volunteer to coordinate a cross-team project, ask to shadow the target team, take over a weekly KPI report, request a stretch assignment, document measurable wins for the resume, find an internal mentor in the target function, explore an internal transfer. If they already hold the first role, focus on what positions them for the next step.
- next_90_days: 3 to 5 learning or job-search actions they can start now. Do not repeat current_job_moves.
